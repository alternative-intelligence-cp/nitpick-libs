## 8. Watch for

- **Do not help the worker.** The point of the run is what happens without a
  hand on it. Intervene only at a stop, and record that you did.
- **A guard refusal during the run is the most valuable finding it can
  produce.** Record the exact command and the reason before fixing anything.
- **The compiler session may be running a harness.** The pin procedure is
  what makes that irrelevant to the worker; if the pin step itself stalls on
  "NOT YET", wait, do not copy a half-written binary.
